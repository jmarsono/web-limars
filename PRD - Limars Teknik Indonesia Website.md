## 1. Executive Summary

PT. Limars Teknik Indonesia is a company specializing in the manufacture and service of commercial and residential kitchen heating equipment. Their product range includes traditional stoves and ovens from various cultural origins as well as modern commercial-grade ovens. Beyond manufacturing, the company provides end-to-end services including restaurant kitchen set design, ducting systems for smoke exhaust and air circulation, gas installation, well drilling, and electrical services for restaurants, homes, and buildings.

This PRD outlines the requirements for developing a professional, modern company website that effectively presents the company's portfolio, services, and brand identity to potential customers and business partners.

## 4. Brand Identity & Design System

### 4.1 Color Palette

The website design must use the brand colors derived from the company logo:

| Color Name       | Hex Code    | Usage                                                        |
|------------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Dark Blue**    | `#1B3A5C`   | Primary brand color — headers, navigation bar, footer, CTAs  |
| **Light Blue**   | `#5BA4D9`   | Accent color — buttons, highlights, hover states, icons      |
| **Gray**         | `#6B7B8D`   | Body text, secondary elements, dividers, subtle backgrounds  |
| **Light Gray**   | `#F0F3F6`   | Section backgrounds, card backgrounds, alternating sections   |
| **White**        | `#FFFFFF`   | Main background, content areas                               |
| **Dark Gray**    | `#2D3436`   | Primary heading text, paragraph text                         |

### 4.2 Typography

| Element         | Font Family                | Weight     | Size (Desktop) |
|-----------------|----------------------------|------------|----------------|
| Headings (H1)   | Inter / Poppins            | Bold (700) | 48px           |
| Headings (H2)   | Inter / Poppins            | Semi-Bold (600) | 36px      |
| Headings (H3)   | Inter / Poppins            | Semi-Bold (600) | 24px      |
| Body Text        | Inter / Open Sans          | Regular (400) | 16px        |
| Small Text       | Inter / Open Sans          | Regular (400) | 14px        |
| Button Text      | Inter / Poppins            | Semi-Bold (600) | 16px      |

### 4.3 Design Principles

- **Professional & Trustworthy** — Clean, corporate feel that conveys reliability in engineering and manufacturing
- **Modern & Industrial** — A blend of modern web aesthetics with industrial strength, reflecting the company's manufacturing roots
- **Mobile-First Responsive** — Fully responsive design optimized for all devices
- **Image-Heavy** — Rich use of product photography and project visuals to showcase quality craftsmanship
- **Clear CTAs** — Prominent call-to-action buttons on every page guiding users toward inquiry/contact

## 13. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk                                          | Impact  | Mitigation Strategy                                                  |
|-----------------------------------------------|---------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Lack of product photography                   | High    | Hire professional photographer or request existing photos from client |
| Delayed content from client                   | Medium  | Set content submission deadlines in project timeline                  |
| Scope creep (additional feature requests)      | Medium  | Strict change management process, separate Phase 2 backlog           |
| Slow hosting or poor performance              | Medium  | Use CDN, optimize images, choose reliable cloud hosting              |
| Low traffic post-launch                       | Medium  | SEO strategy + Google My Business + social media integration         |
